# Simulation — `web/js/sim/`

Pure ES modules. No THREE, no DOM, no npm. Runs in the browser (`import { createGame } from './js/sim/index.js'`) and under `node --test`. All randomness comes from one seeded PRNG (`rng.js`, mulberry32), so a seed replays the same world, the same folk and the same letters.

```
node --test tests/sim/                 # 133 tests (tests/sim/index.js runs every *.test.mjs; Node 22 cannot glob a directory itself)
npm run test:sim                       # the same, as the glob form: node --test 'tests/sim/*.test.mjs'
node tests/sim/play.mjs [seed]         # headless run: the arrival (fleets, introductions, the click, a talk) + the three acts, with a readable log
node tests/sim/browser-check.mjs       # the sim in headless Chrome through a static server (port 8931): the browser-check page + the arrival and the three acts in-page
tests/sim/browser-check.html           # serve the repo root and open it: prints SIM_OK … when the modules run in a browser
```

## Arrival, fleets, the minister, jobs, ventures, talk (ART_DIRECTION §11, 2026-10-04)
New modules: `fleets.js`, `jobs.js`, `ventures.js`, `talk.js` (all pure; tests `tests/sim/fleets|jobs|ventures|talk.test.mjs`).

### Fleets and the minister's election (`fleets.js`, `FLEET`)
- **`game.spawnAll()`** emits the spawns and then **forms the fleets** (`spawnAll({ fleets: false })` skips it). `groupFolk` groups the alive folk by trade into `FLEET_GROUPS`: builders · farmers · bakers · couriers (scout, courier, trader) · scholars (diplomat, artist, dreamer) · crafters; a group under `minPerFleet` 2 merges into its fallback (crafters → builders …), a group over `maxPerFleet` 4 splits (`builders-1`, "The Builders II"). The twelve settlers make 5 fleets.
- **Formation**: each fleet is a square grid (`squareSlots`: `ceil(√n)` columns, `spacing` 1.6 m, centred, the best member in front); the squares stand on an **arc** of `arcRadius` 7.5 m round the camera focus (default `state.spawn`, the landing spot), spread over `arcSpan` 150° on the camera side (+z), pulled in off the water / the plot's edge. `fleet:form { fleets:[{ id, name, trade, members:[agentId], centre:{x,z}, slots:[{agentId,x,z}], caption }], focus }`. Members get `agent:task walk` with `formation:true` to their slot, then stand there (`agent:task idle` with `formation:true, fleetId`; sim task kind `stand`). **`state.fleetHold`** keeps everyone in the squares (no camp work, no wandering, no bubbles, no ventures); a spoken build still pulls its crew out, and `rest` still happens.
- **`game.introduceFleets({ every = 3.4, first = 1.0 })`** → returns the order (fleet ids) and schedules, on `state.fleetBeats`: `fleet:intro { order, every, first, seconds }`, then per fleet `fleet:introduce { fleetId, index, total, name, trade, caption ("The Builders · two flits, two floaties"), members, centre }` with each member hopping 0.3 s apart (`agent:listen { agentId, hop:true }`), then **`election:ask { kind:'minister', candidates:[agentIds], fleets, text:'Choose your minister: click one of them.' }`** (`state.election = { kind, open:true, candidates }`, shown in the snapshot as `election`). Idempotent. If nobody calls it, the sim runs it by itself `autoIntroAfter` 14 s after `fleet:form`.
- **`game.electMinister(agentId)`** = `apply({ type:'appoint_minister', agentId })`. The appoint handler (any source: the click, "make Olla our minister", a reply) closes an open ask: `election:result { kind, agentId, by:'sovereign', asked }`, **`ceremony:start { kind:'minister', agentId, name, where:{x,z}, seconds: 5 }`**, every other folk gets mood +5 (the bridge hops at ≥ +4), the minister says a bubble line, then `ceremony:end` and **`fleet:release { released }`** (`state.fleetHold = false`, the squares break and everyone goes to work). The acceptance letter comes as before. A later appointment (no ask open) is plain: no ceremony. `minister:set` now carries `{ agentId, by:'sovereign'|'crowd', prev }`.
- **Safety nets**: no click for `holdMax` 90 s after the ask → the squares break anyway (the ask stays open, `snapshot.election.open`); no minister after `crowdAfter` 150 s → the crowd votes. A minister who strikes or leaves drops the seal (`minister:set { agentId:null, left }`) and the crowd votes `crowdOnLeave` 8 s later.
- **`game.crowdElection('minister')`** → every folk votes (`voteScore`: the candidate's diplomacy × 0.35 + mood/40 + loyalty/80 + `bond` × 0.8; bonds: same fleet +1, same roof +1, same workplace +0.8, gossiped about +0.5, the sitting minister +1.2 for loyal voters / −0.6 for stubborn ones; ambitious folk vote for themselves, the others never do; a small jitter). Appoints the winner silently (no acceptance letter) and sends **`L.electionResult`** (kind `election_result`, from the Ministry, with the tally), `election:result { kind, agentId, by:'crowd', votes:{id:n}, voters, prev, ballots }`. Only `'minister'` is a kind the crowd elects.
- Newcomers join the fleet of their trade (`joinFleet`); `game.fleetOf(agentId)`; agents carry `fleetId`; the snapshot carries `fleets:[{id,name,members}]` and `agents[].fleet`.

### Jobs and the camp economy (`jobs.js`, `JOB`)
- **Every folk always has a job**: `a.job` (a label, refreshed every 0.5 s and before every snapshot, `snapshot.agents[].work`) and `a.workplaceId` (= `jobId`). The minister is `minister`; a crew member is `builder at the House site`; a worker at a finished place is `<role> at the <name>` (role by kind: farm → farmer, bakery → baker, windmill → miller, dock → fisher, quarry → quarrier, market → trader …, else by the entry's skill); a venture's owner is `keeper of the Tea House`; everyone else works **the camp**: `forager at the camp` (farmers, bakers, dreamers, artists, diplomats) or `gatherer at the camp` (`campRoleFor`, stored as `a.campRole`).
- **The camp**: `state.camp = { forage:[{x,z}×≤3], gather:[{x,z}×≤3] }` (`campSpots`: the lake-shore points nearest the middle, and three dry spots a short walk from it). A folk with no building job loops: `agent:task gather { camp:true, role, to }` (a trip to a spot) → `work { camp:true, role, spot, to:null }` for `gatherSeconds` 7 → `haul { camp:true, carrying:'bread' (forager) | 'crate' (gatherer), product:true }` to the stockpile → a breath of 1.5–4 s → again, with a stroll instead now and then (`wanderChance`). Resting, strikes, meetings and courier runs interrupt it as before.
- **Production per day = each worker's skill × mood at their workplace** (`workerEff`, as before for buildings). The camp's trickle is `rates.camp` in `productionRates` / `state.rates`: `campFood` 0.35 food per forager-day, `campWood` 0.45 + `campStone` 0.25 per gatherer-day, each × eff. A camp of twelve makes ~2.4 food a day and eats 12: the hunger arc (the minister's suggestion, the farm) is intact, only a minute later. `summary().advice` ignores the camp when it says "put folk to work".
- **The minister** never hauls: between duties they `inspect` (`agent:task walk` to a site / a building / the crates, then `idle` with `inspect:true, buildingId` for `inspectSeconds` 6).

### Visible building (`tasks.js`)
- **A site's crew is a crowd**: `crewSize(need) = clamp(need + 2, TASK.crewMin 3, TASK.crewMax 6)`. `assignWorkers` fills it at placement (builders by trade first, then skill fit, the nearest first; `fit = skill + 2.5·builder − dist/12`), the restaff loop tops crews up to `crewSize` whenever anyone is free, and **an uncrewed site never waits**: free folk at once, else `poachHelpers` takes hands off the fullest crew (anyone beyond that site's nominal workers), at placement and every 2 s. Camp workers have no `jobId`, so they are the free pool: a build empties the camp first.
- A design still being drawn (`awaiting_design`) keeps its full crew hauling and hammering at the stakes (site prep) until `designArrived`; progress parks at 1 until then.
- Refusals: a helper's no is silent (only refusals that leave a site under its nominal `workers` emit `agent:refuse` / a letter), and a folk who refused is left alone for `TASK.refusalMemory` 30 s (one letter per burst, not one per site). The headless check (`tests/sim/jobs.test.mjs`): during a house build at least two folk are on the site throughout and nobody stands idle for 5 s.

### Entrepreneurs (`ventures.js`, `VENTURE`)
- From `firstAfter` 150 s, every `every` 70–130 s (never while the squares stand, on the moon or in a meeting; `maxOpen` 1 proposal at a time, `maxVentures` 4 a game, `cooldown` 300 s per folk, one venture each), an **eligible** folk (`ambitious`, or main skill ≥ 8 with mood ≥ 70; mood ≥ 50, not the minister, not striking) proposes an idea from `VENTURE_IDEAS[trade]` (baker: tea house / bun cart; builder: boat workshop / stone yard; farmer: flower stall / olive press; crafter: pottery / lantern workshop; trader: spice stall; scout: kite shop; diplomat: letter office; artist / dreamer: puppet theatre / paint shop …).
- **Asking**: `agent:say { kind:'venture', text:"I would like to open a tea house. May I?" }` + `L.ventureLetter` (kind `venture`, options yes / no → `effects { type:'venture', ventureId, decision }`), `venture:propose { ventureId, agentId, title, request, letterId }`. **Yes** → mood +8, a bubble, **`startVenture`**: `apply({ type:'build', kind:null, request, name:title, at: near their home (else where they stand), assign:[owner] })`, so a catalogue noun becomes that prefab and anything else goes through `building:needsDesign` → codegen like every creation (the mock stock is the fallback); the building carries `venture { id, ownerId, title }`, the owner is on the crew and helpers follow (`venture:start { ventureId, agentId, buildingId, title, x, z, generated }`). **No** → `declined`, mood −3, "Maybe next spring." (`venture:decline`). **Rarely** (`selfStart` 15 %, `small` ideas only) they simply start it: an informing letter (`meta.started`, no options), a bubble, the site at once.
- **Done** (`onVentureDone`): the owner keeps it (`takeJob`, job `keeper of the …`, mood +6), a generated design with no `perDay` earns `defaultPerDay` { coin 2, goods 1 }, `venture:done`, "The tea house is open. Come by!". `state.ventures[{ id, agentId, title, request, small, status: proposed|approved|building|started|done|declined, letterId, buildingId }]`; the snapshot shows the last three. Tests drive it with `game.proposeVenture(agentId, idea?, { ask: true|false })`.

### Talk and speech bubbles (`talk.js`, `SAY`)
- **`game.talkContext(agentId, text)`** → the body for `POST /api/talk`: `{ agentId, agent:{ id, name, species, trade, traits, mood, moodWord, energy, loyalty, skills, known, job, status, homeless, isMinister, fleet, memory:[recent events], campRole }, text, history:[{you, me}], snapshot }`.
- **`game.talk(agentId, text, result?)`** → `{ ok, reply, mood, action, applied }`. With a server `result` (`{ reply, mood?, action? }`) the reply is taken as is (trimmed to two short sentences, `trimReply`; the mood clamped ±5); without one the **offline mind `mockTalk`** answers (the same engine the server's mock uses): intents greeting · how are you · who are you · what do you do · skills · what do you want · the town · the minister · the neighbours · food · home · fleet · thanks · rest · build · bye · yes / no, each with plain, trait-coloured lines (proud boasts, timid hedges, gossips hint, hungry / homeless folk say so). The exchange is remembered (`a.memory.talks`, last 6; `a.memory.events`, last 8: "helped raise the house", "the sovereign chose me as minister", "opened my tea house" …), `agent:listen` fires, and **`agent:say { agentId, text, kind:'talk', ttl }`** carries the bubble. An `action` is applied: `build` (near the folk, the folk on the crew), `rest`, `assign { to }`, `ask_crowd`, `call_meeting`.
- **Spontaneous one-liners**: from `firstAfter` 24 s, every 14–26 s (≈ 3 a minute, never while the squares stand, in a meeting or on the moon) one folk says a line fitted to the moment (`spontaneousLine`: hunger first, then strikes, rest, the minister, a site, a venture, no roof, the camp, traits, species) → `agent:say { kind:'idle', ttl: 4.5 }`. Events add a few more: a crew member on `building:done` (`kind:'done'`), the new minister (`'ceremony'`), ventures (`'venture'`). Letters stay for formal matters.

## Minds: real AI agents (ART_DIRECTION §18, 2026-10-04) — see `docs/minds.md`
Modules `minds.js` (pure: personas, memory, relationships, intents, conversations, the director's effects, the mocks) and `mindloop.js` (the scheduler the game layer attaches: `game.attachMinds(createMindLoop(game, { call, visible, paused, mode }))`; `game.tick` drives `loop.update()`). Tests `tests/sim/minds.test.mjs`.
- **Personas** (`state.personas[id]`: backstory, voice, quirks, values, fear, goal, opinions, secret) land once from the cast (`game.castRequest()` → POST /api/minds/cast → `game.castPersonas(cast)`; `game.castPersonas()` alone = the mock cast, 16 hand-written seeds, one each, with `findTensions` rivals / friends / crush / grudge). `game.personaOf(id)`.
- **Memory** per folk (`agent.mind.memory`, importance 1–5, ≤ 40; `agent.mind.reflections` ≤ 6): `game.observe(id, text, { imp, kind, about })`; the sim observes by itself (`wireObservations`); every 8 items `game.reflect(id, text?)`. **Relationships** `state.relationships['a|b'].a` −1..1: `game.affinity(a, b)`, `game.nudgeAffinity(a, b, delta, why)`, `game.relationships()`; they feed the crowd's vote (`fleets.bond`).
- **Think**: `game.thinkRequest(id)` (persona + state + memory + relationships + world + the action vocabulary) → `game.applyIntent(id, answer, { source })` executes `work · rest · wander_to · talk_to · help · complain · propose_venture · write_letter · vote · refuse · celebrate · steal · gossip` through the sim (tasks, letters, ventures, conflicts, moods, affinities); `game.mockThink(id)` is the offline / rules mind. Emits `agent:intent` and `agent:say { kind:'mind' }`.
- **Conversations**: a `talk_to` / `gossip` / `help` intent starts one (`state.conversations`, `conversation:start`); `game.converseRequest(id)` → POST /api/minds/converse (2–4 alternating Haiku turns) → `game.applyConversation(id, { lines, outcome })`: the lines go out as `agent:say { kind:'chat', conversationId, turn, of }` 2.6 s apart (`tickMinds`, the say queue), the outcome moves memories and affinity and may spawn a petition or a quarrel; `conversation:lines` / `conversation:end`.
- **Director**: `game.directRequest(reason)` → POST /api/minds/direct → `game.applyDirection(answer)`: conflicts, visitors (a nation's letter by envoy), weather (`world:weather`), festivals (`festival`), opportunities (a venture proposal), goal nudges, the minister's briefing letter (`kind:'report'`, `meta.kind:'briefing'`); `game.mockDirect(reason)` offline. `state.minds { cast, directions, arc, milestones, tensions, weather, sayQueue }`.
- **Talk**: `talkContext` carries `persona` and `mind { memory, reflections, goal, relationships }` after the cast (the server answers on the persona model); offline the mock reply takes the persona's voice. `game.talk` records the exchange in the memory and pokes the loop.
- **Snapshot**: `agents[].goal`, `minds: { cast, directions, arc, weather?, bonds }`. All of it is plain JSON in `game.state` (personas, relationships, conversations, minds, `agents[].mind`).
- **The loop** (`createMindLoop`): 30–40 s per folk staggered, 1–3 s after a trigger, ≤ 40 calls/min, 3 in flight, 5 s think timeout, the rules (`mockThink`, `source:'rules'`) on any failure with backoff, a minute on the rules after five failures in a row, paused while hidden / on a menu / on the moon / in the squares / in a meeting; the director at the start, every `directorEveryMs`, at milestones (first building, election, first conflict, institution) and on `loop.direct('demand')`; `loop.status()` / `mind:status`.

## Conflicts and institutions (ART_DIRECTION §15, 2026-10-04)
Module `conflicts.js` (pure; tests `tests/sim/conflicts.test.mjs`). Rule-based offline; the live minds only write the letters in richer words (`/api/letter` purpose `conflict`).

### Conflicts emerge from the state, the traits and the relationships (`CONFLICT`)
- **Kinds** (`CONFLICT_KINDS`) and when they can come: **theft** at the stockpile when food is short (`food < 2 × pop` or hungry; the thief is the lazy / ambitious / low-loyalty one, the witness a gossip; 3 food leaves the crates at once and again on every escalation), **quarrel** between rivals (same trade, both ambitious, both proud, a stubborn one, the same workplace, worse in a low mood; topic: "who is the better baker" / "who should lead the next site" / "who gets the credit for the house"), **noise** (a folk whose home stands within 9 m of a workshop / tavern / windmill / quarry / woodcutter / market / bakery…), **land** (two ambitious folk claim the same plot by the nearest building), **neglect** (a lazy or worn-out worker lets a finished workplace go), **envoy** (a cold nation, attitude < 45, complains that our best flier flew over their nets; `neighbourId`), **jealousy** of the minister (an ambitious / proud folk with mood < 70 or loyalty < 55).
- **Shape**: `{ id:'c1', kind, parties:[agentIds], roles:{ offender?, complainant? }, place:{x,z}, severity 1-3, since (s), day, status:'open'|'handled'|'resolved'|'escalated', summary (one plain sentence, < 120 chars), topic, buildingId?, neighbourId?, letterId, how?, howText?, by?, ignored, escalations }`. `state.conflicts` keeps them all; the snapshot shows the unresolved ones (`conflictsBrief`).
- **Rate**: nothing before `firstAfter` 200 s of play; then one every `every` 120–200 s (× `unhappyFactor` 0.55 when the average mood is below 50 or the town is hungry); at most `maxOpen` 3 unresolved at a time; never while the squares stand, in a meeting or on the moon; the same kind twice in a row is unlikely (× 0.4). Candidates are weighted (theft 3 × the institutions' factor × 2.5 when hungry, quarrel 2, noise 1.5, land 1.2, neglect 1–1.5, envoy by coldness, jealousy 0.8) and one is drawn. Tested: 4–14 conflicts in 15 hungry minutes with every one answered, fewer in a content town.
- **Start**: parties' mood −3 (the offender −1), the complainant says a line (`agent:say kind:'conflict'`), the **minister writes** (`L.conflictLetter`, kind `conflict`, from the minister, or the Ministry when no minister sits), `conflict:start { conflict }`.
- **The letter**: subject by kind, the summary, one detail, and the **minister's advice in their traits' voice** (proud: punish it and be seen; timid: a quiet word; generous: feed them; ambitious: give me a patrol / a court to command; stubborn: I have said before, a patrol; gossip: not the first time; lazy: could it wait; loyal: whatever you decide). Options are the quick replies, executable by the parser, with `meta.quick: 3` (the in-world tag shows the first three; the inbox all): theft → talk to them · punish the thief · start a police patrol · build a granary · ignore it; quarrel → talk · set up a court · punish them both · ignore; noise → talk · start a night watch · compensate them · ignore; land → talk · set up a court · ignore; neglect → talk · punish them · open a school · ignore; envoy → compensate them · talk · ignore; jealousy → talk · start a council · ignore. `effects.yes` = talk, `effects.no` = ignore, so a plain "yes" / "no" to the letter works; `meta: { kind:'conflict', conflictId, conflictKind, parties, severity, place, agentId? }`.
- **Escalation** (`escalateAfter` 150 s unanswered, `ignoredEscalateAfter` 60 s once the sovereign said ignore): severity +1, status `escalated`, the complainant mood −6 / loyalty −4 (the offender −3 / −2), another theft; **moods are floored at `BAL.hungerFloor` like hunger** (silence sours, it never breaks the demo); at severity 3 the complainant takes −8 and, **if the conflict was ignored (or after `BAL.noLeaveBefore` 15 min)** and their mood is below `strikeEnd`, **strikes** (`consequence:'strike'`, the normal strike letter; it ends when their mood recovers); at severity 3 for `leaveAfter` 420 s more (never before `noLeaveBefore`) the complainant **leaves** (`consequence:'left'`, status resolved, how `left`). `conflict:escalate { conflictId, kind, severity, parties, place, summary, consequence:'mood'|'strike'|'left', agentId? }`.
- **Resolution** (`resolve_conflict { conflictId?, how }`, or the letter's yes / no, or `game.resolveConflict(ref, how)`): `how` is one of `HOWS` talk · punish · compensate · ignore, or free text mapped by `normaliseHow` ("lock him up" → punish, "give them a loaf" → compensate, "let it be" → ignore, anything else → talk). No id, or an unknown one → the newest unanswered conflict (`findConflict`; a kind word or a party's name also finds one). **talk**: every party +4 mood, +2 loyalty (a nation +3 attitude). **punish**: the offender −8 / −5 loyalty (a quarrel: both −4), the complainant +4, everyone else +1 loyalty (justice seen), a nation +5. **compensate**: 3 food (theft, noise) or 3 coin (else; 3 goods / coin to a nation) leave the crates, the complainant +6 (a nation +8). **ignore**: the complainant −3, the conflict stays open, `ignored` (faster escalation, the strike). Handled conflicts become `resolved` after `settleSeconds` 25. The letter is marked resolved (`letter:resolved`). **The minister walks to the place** (`agent:task walk { duty:'settle', conflictId }`, then `idle { duty:'settle' }` for 4 s) unless an institution's member does it. Events: `conflict:handle { conflictId, kind, how, by:'sovereign'|'institution', agentId (who goes there) | null, institutionId?, parties, place }` at once, `conflict:resolve { conflictId, kind, how, by, parties }` 25 s later.

### Institutions (`found_institution`, `INSTITUTION_SPECS`)
`found_institution { kind: 'police patrol' | 'night watch' | 'court' | 'school' | 'guild' | 'festival committee' | free text, members?: n, leader?: agentId | name, name?: title, request?: 'a watchtower' }` (also `game.foundInstitution(opts)`). `institutionKind(text)` maps words to a spec: police / patrol / guards / militia → **patrol**, night watch / watchmen / sentries → **watch**, court / judge / tribunal / law → **court**, school / teach / academy → **school**, guild / union / brotherhood → **guild** (of a trade when one is named: "the bakers' guild" → Bakers' Guild, members = that trade), festival / feast / carnival → **festival**, anything else → **generic** (named after the words: "a lantern choir" → Lantern Choir, a badge from a small palette).

| kind | name · title · badge | members | duty | remit (settles) | effects | schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| patrol | Police Patrol · constable · blue armband | 2 | walks a **route** round the stockpile, the square, the noisy places and the tray, all day (`patrolPause` 2.2 s at each point) | theft, noise (a free member walks to the conflict within ~3 s and settles it) | theft × 0.15, noise × 0.3 | — |
| watch | Night Watch · watchman · lantern | 2 | the same route plus the plot's three far edges, **at night only** (the last third of each 60 s day); by day they keep to the crates | theft, noise | theft × 0.35, noise × 0.5 | — |
| court | Court · judge · black sash | 1 (best diplomacy, loyal, generous) | paces the square (the Assembly's steps when it stands) | quarrel, land, jealousy, envoy | fairness: everyone else +2 mood after a verdict | a **session** every 90 s (first within 40 s) whenever a conflict in the remit is open: the judge and the parties are summoned to the square for 12 s, then every one is settled (`how:'court'`) |
| school | School · teacher · chalk pouch | 1 (art, diplomacy, generous) | the square | neglect | neglect × 0.5 | a **lesson** every 120 s, 14 s: up to 3 free folk come; each one's weakest skill +0.2, mood +1 |
| guild | <Trade>s' Guild · guild member · guild pin | the trade's folk (2–4) | the square | — | quarrels inside a trade × 0.3 | a **meeting** every 150 s, 10 s: members +2 mood |
| festival | Festival Committee · committee member · ribbon | 2 (art, generous) | the square | — | — | a **festival** every 180 s, 18 s at the square: everyone +5 mood, 4 food |
| generic | <the words> · member · a sash / band | 2 | the square | — | — | a **gathering** every 150 s, 8 s: members +2 |

- **Members** (`pickMembers`): alive, not the minister, not already in an institution, not striking / delivering / leaving, scored by the spec's `fit` (skills + traits + mood) minus half the value of the job they would leave (the last farmer is not taken first) plus willingness (`willing`; an unwilling pick is "pressed", mood −2 instead of +3 / +5 for the ambitious). `leader` by id or name wins; else the best. Nobody free → `ok:false` "Nobody is free to form a …" (never a crash). Members **leave their job / site** (`releaseAgent`), get `agent.role = { institutionId, kind, title, badge, leader }`, their job label becomes `constable of the Police Patrol` (`jobs.jobLabel`), and they are **never drafted** to sites, staffed at buildings, conscripted, used as couriers, counted as camp workers or allowed to propose ventures. They still eat, rest, strike, attend meetings and vote. A folk who leaves drops out of the institution (`onAgentGone`); an institution with no members left turns `dormant` (`institution:disband`).
- **Behaviour**: `tasks.plan` asks `game.planDuty(a)` before the camp. Patrol / watch: `agent:task walk { duty:'patrol', institutionId, to }` leg by leg, a 2.2 s `idle { duty:'patrol' }` at each point; `institution:patrol { institutionId, kind, agentId, route:[{x,z}], night }` fires at the start of every round (the visual layer may run its own loop on the route and ignore the per-leg walks, as it does for work roles). Sessions: every attendee gets `walk { duty:'session', institutionId, conflictId? }` to a spot round the venue, then `idle { duty:'session' }` until it ends. Between sessions the other kinds visit the venue every 10–18 s (`walk / idle { duty:'attend' }`). Settling: `walk { duty:'settle', conflictId, institutionId }` then `idle { duty:'settle' }` 4 s.
- **A place for it**: `request` ("a watchtower", "a courthouse") or a place-like kind (…tower / …house / …hall) also runs the normal build path (`build { kind:null, request, name }` → catalogue prefab or `building:needsDesign` → codegen); the building carries `institution: { id, kind }`, the institution `buildingId`, and sessions move to its door once it stands. Effect `{ type:'institution_site', id, buildingId }`.
- Events: `institution:found { institution }` (the whole record: id, kind, name, text, badge, title, members, leader, founded, day, status, buildingId, trade), then per member `institution:assign { institutionId, kind, agentId, role:{ institutionId, kind, title, badge, leader }, uniform:{ institution: kind, badge, title, leader } }`, `agent:say { kind:'institution' }` from the leader, a Ministry letter (`L.institutionLetter`, kind `ministry`, `meta.kind:'institution'`), a toast; `institution:act { kind:'settle'|'session'|'verdict'|'session_end'|'lesson'|'lesson_end'|'festival'|'festival_end'|'meeting'|'meeting_end'|'gathering'|'gathering_end', institutionId, agentId (the leader / the settler), agentIds?, where?, seconds?, conflictId?, conflictIds? }`; `institution:disband { institutionId, kind, name }`.
- `state.institutions[{ id:'i1', kind, name, text, badge, title, members:[ids], leader, founded, day, status:'active'|'dormant', buildingId, trade, nextActAt, nextSessionAt, session:{ kind, where, endsAt, members, conflictIds } | null, acts }]`.

## Rewards: every creation gives something back (ART_DIRECTION §22b, 2026-10-04) — `rewards.js`
Four areas, no more: **money** (coin), **happiness** (smiling lantern), **science** (telescope / flask), **civ** (rising banner). `state.areas = { money, happiness, science, civ }` is the tally; `game.rewards` is the API.
- **When:** `game.completeBuilding` → `building:done` → **`reward:gain`** (then `refreshDerived`, so a level-up's `reward:milestone` follows the gain). Fills (field / forest / garden / plaza / vineyard / orchard) and lines (road / wall / fence / river) are buildings too, so they pay on completion; `institution:found` pays as a creation as well. A building pays once (`b.rewarded`).
- **`reward:gain { source:'building'|'institution', buildingId|null, institutionId?, agentId?, kind, name, x, z, generated, novel, category, shape:'point'|'area'|'line', gains:{money?, happiness?, science?, civ}, totals, milestone? }`** — `x, z` is where the tokens rise from; `totals = { money, happiness, science, civ, level, levelIndex, levelNext, levelProgress (0..1 toward the next stage, from the prosperity thresholds), prosperity }` is what the tally shows; `milestone {area, at, text}` is on the gain that crossed a line.
- **`reward:milestone { area, at, text, totals, levelUp? }`** — money / happiness / science at 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 200, once each (`state.rewards.passed`), with a short line ("Happiness 25: the folk are content"); **civ's milestones are the level-ups**: on every `stage` event, `{ area:'civ', at:<stage>, text:'Civ: a hamlet now: the first lanes', levelUp:{from, to} }` (the bigger animated moment).
- **The table** (`REWARD_TABLE`): money / happiness / science for every catalogue id, every starter-library prefab (manifest.js), the fills and the lines; `INSTITUTION_REWARDS` per institution kind (patrol, watch, court, school, guild, festival, generic). **Civ** is derived from the entry's prosperity `value` (× 0.5, 1..15), so the civ tally is literally the progress toward the next level. A drawn outline adds +1 to the main area per 80 m² (max +3); a drawn stroke +1 per 20 m (max +2). A test asserts the table covers every id.
- **Generated things** are classified by meaning: the codegen meta's optional `areas: {money, happiness, science}` (0–5, in `server/prompts/codegen.md`, `CODEGEN_SCHEMA`, kept by `normaliseCodegenMeta` and the sim's `coerceMeta` as `entry.areas`) wins; else `classifyWords(name + noun + request + aliases + desc, category)` (the head noun counts double; a landmark always gets happiness ≥ 1; no hit → the category's base: landmark happiness 3 science 1, building money 2, prop / nature happiness 1, farm / animal money 2); the **first of a generated kind adds science +1** ("we learned to build it", `state.rewards.learned`). A lighthouse → science 3 happiness 1 (+1 science when novel); a rubber duck → happiness 3.
- **Prosperity headline:** `computeProsperity` adds `ECO.areasWeight 0.3` per point of money + happiness + science (civ is the value sum it already counts). One creation adds < 34 prosperity in all, so no stage is ever skipped by a reward.
- **API:** `game.rewards.totals()`, `game.rewards.preview('a lighthouse' | 'house')` → the gains a request would give (for a hint before building), `game.rewards.gainsFor(b)`, `AREAS`, `AREA_META` (label, icon, colour), `REWARD` (the knobs). The snapshot and `game.summary()` carry `areas`. Browser check: `node tests/sim/rewards-browser.mjs --url http://localhost:<port>` (shots/sim/rewards-*.png).

## Species (ART_DIRECTION §7)
- **Our settlers are only the two fliers of the Red arch reference: `flit`** (round pistachio-green, propeller cap) **and `floatie`** (marshmallow in a striped bathing suit under a parasol). `OUR_SPECIES = ['flit', 'floatie']`; `SPECIES` (the legacy export) is the same list. They both **walk and fly**: the sim moves them abstractly, the agents bridge walks them for work and flies them to travel.
- **Twelve settlers, six of each**, shuffled. **Either species holds any trade**: the main skills are dealt from a fixed hand (`SETTLER_SKILLS`: 3 × building, 2 × baking, 2 × farming, diplomacy, scouting, trading, art, crafting), so every seed has ≥ 3 builders, 2 bakers who can answer "who's good at baking?", a scout to carry letters, and **Olla, the best diplomat** (flit or floatie, diplomacy ≥ 8, trade `diplomat`), for "make Olla our minister". Hidden skills, traits and the proud / timid answers are unchanged. `makeFolk(rng, { species?, skill?, name? })`: our species lean only **softly** (`LEANING[sp].soft`: 30 % the leaning, 15 % its alt, the rest any skill); the walkers' species keep firm leanings for envoys and visitors. Trade names: `tradeOf(species, skill)` (a floatie whose art is best is a `dreamer`, a flit whose scouting is best a `scout`, every other skill its plain trade).
- **Newcomers** (immigration) are always ours: the species we have fewer of, with a main skill weighted toward what the town lacks (`makeNewcomer`). Their letter says they flew in.
- **Couriers** (`pickCourier(agents, can)`): the `scout` / `courier` by trade first, then the idle folk with the best scouting, then the best scout among whoever `can`. Used for letters (`queueLetter`) and gifts (`sendGift`). Nobody at all → the 2.5 s fallback / a carrier-less journey, as before.
- **The nations' peoples are the five walkers** (`NEIGHBOUR_SPECIES`): n1 Sorrento-on-the-Rock = **The Drop Riviera** (`peoples: ['drop']`), n2 Little Lantern = **The Loaf Republic** (`['loaf']`), n3 Grey Harbour = **The Puffer Harbour** (`['puffer', 'pip', 'scoot']`, leader a puffer, gruff). `envoySpecies(n, rng)` picks the leader's people 60 % of the time, the others otherwise; every `envoy:send` carries `species`, and the election's carries `speciesOf: { n1, n2, n3 }` so the visual layer can fly a drop, a loaf and a puffer. `findNeighbour` answers to any of a nation's peoples ("the scoots" → n3, "the loaves" → n2); **"the flits" / "the floaties" name nobody** (they are us), so `pickNeighbour` falls back to the last nation mentioned. The snapshot's `neighbours[]` carries `peoples`.
- **Letters**: neighbours speak of "our loaves / drops / puffers" (`peopleOf(n)`) and of "your flits … a floatie … parasol-first into our nets", the greeting saw us "come down out of the sky on propellers and parasols"; our folk sign "from somewhere up high" (flit) / "drifting, as ever" (floatie), newcomers "wound up the propeller cap and flew" / "let the wind take the parasol", a leaver is "in the air over the water". The minister's report says "the builders are restless", never "the puffers".
- `game.setNations(places.nations)` still adopts the globe's **positions**, but **not a title that names one of our species** (`staleTitle`): the globe's old "The Flit Sky-hold" leaves the sim's "The Puffer Harbour" alone. `web/js/globe/geography.js` n3 (`name`, `species: 'flit'`, the pistachio `skyhold` style) still needs the globe builder's rename to match.

### Creation: what "build" does with words
`build { kind, request, name?, at, count?, assign? }`
1. **The noun, not the tail.** An explicit `kind` that resolves in the catalogue is trusted. Otherwise the request's **noun alone** is resolved, with its location tail stripped (`on | at | by | near | next to | in | into | beside | behind | in front of | across | over | under | around | along | above | below | towards | for | there | here …`): "a lighthouse near the house" is a lighthouse (needs a design), never a house; "a wind mill over there" is a windmill; "a hut in the lake" is a hut, floating. Leading verbs and articles are dropped ("let's build a", "put a", "another"). `kind:'pending:<slug>'` (as the snapshot shows an undesigned thing) and "another one" / "one more" / "the same again" repeat the last creation, pending designs included (one Ministry letter, and the design lands on every sibling).
2. **Category** (`inferCategory`): the head noun decides — duck / bench / boat / lantern / cat … are **props** (free, 1 worker, 12 s, 2×2, value 5); tree / rock / bush / flower … are **nature** (free, 15 s, 3×3); lighthouse / statue / tower / castle / dragon / rocket … are **landmarks** (5w 9s 2c, 2 workers, 60 s, 5×5, **value 30**: each generated landmark is a stage's worth of prosperity); everything else is a building (value 16). A generated asset keeps the request's category unless its `meta.category` says otherwise. `effects[0]` and `building:needsDesign` carry `noun`, `category` and `size` ('giant' | 'big' | 'small' | 'normal') for the sketch / codegen prompt.
3. **Where** (`resolveAt` + `resolveAnchor`): the LLM's `at` wins when it is specific (`pointer` with x/z, `near` with a ref, `center`, `water`). Otherwise the request's tail decides: "in / on / into the lake | pond | water" → **on the water** (`mode:'water', water:'lake'`; a pointer into the lake with those words floats near the pointer instead of being nudged ashore); "in the sea | bay | ocean" → on the sea just off the plot; "by / near the lake" → the lake shore; "on the cliff" / "by the sea" / "on the coast | shore | beach" → the plot's sea edge; "in the middle | square | centre" → the centre; "near / next to / behind / in front of the X" → `findBuilding(X)` (id, kind, name, noun or a word of the name: "the duck" finds the Giant Rubber Duck; the newest wins), or a nation (the plot edge facing it). Things that float by nature (duck, boat, swan, raft, buoy, lily, island, whale …; `isWaterThing`) go on the lake when nothing else is said. Also accepted: `at.mode:'water'` with `water:'lake'|'sea'` and optional x/z (an addition to §4's modes; the server schema should list it, but the sim infers it from the words anyway).
3b. **At a mark** (`at.mode:'mark'`, ART_DIRECTION §5: placement is never arbitrary). The mark is `at.mark` (the orchestrator attaches `marks.current()` to the action; the server already puts the full mark it received there), else `game.state.mark` (`game.setMark`); neither → the pointer x/z if given, else auto. `game.markSpot`:
   - **point** → the centre is the point, exactly. Blocked (another building, the water for a land thing, off the plot) → the **smallest move that fits**: rings of `MARK.step` 0.5 m up to `MARK.maxNudge` 8 m, nearest angle first, reported as `effects[{type:'nudge', id, from, to, dist, why:'overlap'|'water'|'edge'}]` and `b.nudge`, logged "nudged 1.4 m off the mark: overlap". Nothing within 8 m → `ok:false` "No room … within 8 m of your mark" (never a far relocation). A point inside a field / forest is honoured (areas never block a marked point; the overlap is on record as `{type:'overlaps', id, ids}`). A thing that floats by nature (`isWaterThing` / `entry.water`) floats exactly there when the mark is on water, and sits on the land when it is not.
   - **area** → an area kind (`shape:'area'`) fills it: `b.shape = { poly, bbox, centroid, areaM2 }`, `x/z` = the centroid, `footprint` = the bbox (w×d), placed exactly (what it crosses is in `overlaps`). A point kind named for an area ("a house", "a statue") sits at the centroid with its footprint shrunk to fit the outline (never grown), `shape.fit = true`, the same small nudge if blocked.
   - **line** → any kind follows the stroke: `b.shape = { pts, bbox, length, width }`, `x/z` = the midpoint along the stroke, `rot` = its heading there (`-atan2(dz, dx)`, three.js y-rotation), footprint = the bbox for a line kind (its own for a point kind such as a bridge).
   - `count > 1` at a mark: the first fills the mark, the rest spiral out from it. Effects: `site` / `needsDesign` first (with `shape`), then `{type:'mark', used:true, kind, id, markId?, exact, areaM2?|length?}` so the orchestrator can `marks.consume(markId)`, then `nudge` / `overlaps`. `state.mark` is cleared once used. An area / line kind placed **without** a mark gets a default rectangle outline (`shape.rect`) / straight strip (`shape.straight`) of its footprint, so the visual layer always has a polygon or polyline to fill. A design arriving for a shaped site never moves it. The snapshot shows `shape:'area'|'line'` on such buildings.
   - **Folk work the shape**: `siteSpot` returns a fresh low-discrepancy point inside the outline for every leg (hauls, work trips), and a point beside the stroke (half its width + 0.6 m, on the side the folk come from) spread along a line.
4. **Never blocked.** Creation is never blocked on resources or the stage (CONCEPT priority 6): the crates pay what they hold, the rest is forgiven with a `{type:'short'}` effect and a throttled Ministry note; a stage-locked kind lands with `{type:'locked'}`. The only hard gates are **no ground** (land for land things, water for floating ones) and the **Assembly** (town only). `count` 1..6 repeats; a partial count is `ok:true` with a `{type:'partial'}` effect.
5. **A crew always comes.** Free folk by skill then willingness; nobody free → production workers are drafted (thinnest job first); nobody willing at all → after 20 s the Ministry **conscripts** the least unwilling folk (mood −3, a "Hands found for the X" letter) so a spoken thing is always raised. A floating thing is worked from the shore (`b.workSpot`); folk never walk into the lake.

### Actions (§4) — the rest
- `ask_crowd` — `skill` or inferred from the question; 1-3 folk answer by letter (proud over-claim, timid under-claim). Sets `known[skill]` / `claims[skill]`.
- `appoint_minister` — by id or name (prefix ok). Mood +8, loyalty +10, `minister:set`, acceptance letter.
- `assign` — `agentIds` (array or one id), `to`: building id / name / kind / word of its name, `'idle'` or `'rest'`. Each folk passes `willing()`; refusals write letters with options.
- `reply_letter` — marks resolved; applies `effects[decision]` (mood, loyalty, assign, force_assign, build, festival, resources, attitude, trade, gift → `send_gift`, ally, ally_ask, go_moon). Missing `letterId` = the latest open letter; an unknown id is refused. Replying to a nation sets `lastNeighbour`.
- `demolish` — refunds half of `paidCost`; releases workers; `building:remove`.
- `call_meeting` — gathers everyone at the Assembly if built, else the centre; minister's report; `meeting:end` after 20 s with minutes.
- `message_agent` — mood +3 and a reply letter.
- `trade` — attitude ≥ 25 and the goods on hand; `give` / `get` as `{ wood: 5 }` or the server schema's `[{ res:'wood', n:5 }]` (`cleanBundle`); attitude +5; sets `lastNeighbour`.
- `name_settlement`, `noop`.
- `found_institution { kind, members?, leader?, name?, request? }` and `resolve_conflict { conflictId?, how }` — §15 above. Reply effects `{ type:'conflict', conflictId, how }` and `{ type:'institution', kind, members?, leader? }` run the same handlers.

### Actions (§9) — diplomacy, the election, the moon

## Balancing numbers (where to tune)
**Demo-friendliness (the economy cannot kill the demo)**
- Starting crates: **food 60** (was 40), wood 30, stone 20, coin 10. Twelve settlers eat 12/day, so hunger starts at ~5 min; at **110 s** the minister (or the Ministry, when no minister) writes a gentle suggestion with "Build a farm / Build a well" options (`SOC.suggestAt`), unless something already feeds the town.
- `BAL.hungerFloor 30` (above `strikeMood 25`): hunger alone never makes anyone strike or leave; hunger letters still come every 120 s as flavour. `BAL.noLeaveBefore 900`: **nobody leaves in the first 15 minutes**, whatever their heart says (tested: 15 idle, unfed minutes with a house and a lighthouse → 0 left, 0 striking).
- Hungry folk refuse a little less readily (willingness −0.15, was −0.25), and a site nobody will work is conscripted after 20 s (`tasks.conscript`).
- Stage thresholds (`ECO.thresholds`): **hamlet 36, village 70, town 115, civilisation 230**. Prosperity = Σ value of done things + population + 10 × housed fraction + mood/10 + 0.5 goods + 0.3 coin + 8 per ally + 6 when elected + 20 once the envoy has returned. The second creation makes a hamlet (~100 s); a ~10 min demo with eight creations, two of them generated landmarks (value 30 each), reaches **town** (tested: house, windmill, lighthouse, duck, dragon statue, farm, bakery, well → town by 10 min, the Assembly unlocked).

## Decisions worth knowing
- The lake is a seeded 14-point blob (≈ 10×7) in one of four off-centre spots; the sea polygon starts 2-3.6 units beyond `z0`. Floating things need their centre ≥ min(1.2, half their smaller side) inside the shore; a 2×2 duck fits almost anywhere on the lake, a 5×5 castle only in its middle; three or four small floating things fill it. "In the sea" places 6 units beyond the plot's sea edge.
- Placement spirals out from the anchor in 1.25-unit rings (0.75 on water); a pointer tries the exact point first. Footprints keep a 1-unit gap (0.8 on water), 0.5 from the plot edge, 0.6 from water. The stockpile, tray and spawn point are reserved.
- The sim does not import `geography.js` (it imports from the sim; keeping the sim a leaf keeps `node --test` and the browser free of a globe ↔ sim cycle). The nation positions are the same numbers by contract; `game.setNations(places.nations)` adopts the globe's if a builder moves them (and a title, unless it names one of our species).
- Species (2026-10-04, ART_DIRECTION §7): the demo's guarantees (3 builders, 2 bakers, a scout, Olla the diplomat) moved from the species roster to a dealt hand of main skills, so flits and floaties hold every trade and the tests check trades (`a.trade === 'builder'`), never species. "The flits" as a nation name is gone: n3 is the Puffer Harbour.
- `designArrived` adds the noun and its head noun as aliases, so "another duck" resolves to the learned `rubber_duck`; the full request is kept as a third alias.
- The passive election is deliberately late (6 min, town) so that in a demo "let's go to the moon" is the normal path (letter in ~3 s, voyage after it), and a town that keeps building gets the letter as a surprise.
- Round-1/2 audit decisions still hold: soft costs, soft stage gate (bar the Assembly), hunger floors mood, `apply` only accepts own keys, `tick` ignores non-finite dts, re-planned work tasks after meetings, idempotent `designArrived`, rescued courier letters.

## What the visual and game layers must handle (§15)
- `agent.role` / `institution:assign { uniform:{ institution:'patrol'|'watch'|'court'|'school'|'guild'|'festival'|'generic', badge:'blue armband'|'lantern'|'black sash'|'chalk pouch'|'guild pin'|'ribbon'|…, title, leader } }` → an additive uniform piece (the agents bridge's identity layer); `institution:disband` or a `role` gone → take it off.
- `agent:task` with `duty:'patrol'` (a walk leg + a short idle at each point) and `institution:patrol { route }` once per round → a patrol look (a slow walk, a glance left and right; a lantern held up at night). `duty:'session'` → folk standing round `where` facing the leader (a judge with the sash; pupils sitting; a festival with hops) for `seconds`; `duty:'settle'` → the constable / the minister walks up to the parties and has a word (`institution:act kind:'settle'` / `conflict:handle`); `duty:'attend'` → a quiet stand at the venue.
- `conflict:start { conflict }` → the complainant's bubble is already an `agent:say kind:'conflict'`; the parties may face each other / stomp (`place` is where it happens: the stockpile, a building, the plot edge). `conflict:escalate` → a bigger huff; `consequence:'strike'` is followed by the normal strike task. `conflict:handle` → the two shake hands (talk / court), the offender is marched off / hangs its head (punish), a basket changes hands (compensate); `conflict:resolve` → back to normal.
- The minister's letter (kind `conflict`) is the tiny tag over the minister's head (§15: the name, the one-line `summary`, and the first `meta.quick` 3 options); the inbox shows all options. `letter:resolved` fires when any reply path answers it. With the live API `desk.js` may rewrite it through `/api/letter` purpose `conflict` with `context: { conflict: letter.meta (id, kind, summary, parties, severity, place), institutions: snapshot.institutions.map(i => i.kind) }` and `agent` = the minister (empty for the Ministry).
- Commands: `found_institution` / `resolve_conflict` go through `game.apply` like any action; effects `{ type:'institution', … }` (a notice "The Police Patrol is founded: X and Y"), `{ type:'institution_site', buildingId }` (a site like any other), `{ type:'conflict', conflictId, how, status, agentId }` (a notice "The minister goes to have a word").
- A building with `b.institution { id, kind }` is an institution's seat (sessions at its door once done).

## Known gaps
- `footprint` of a marked area / line is its **bbox** (as §10 asks). Anything that turns footprints into obstacles (the agents' nav builds round obstacles from `b.footprint`) should skip buildings with `b.shape.poly` / `b.shape.pts`: fields and roads are walkable, and a 30 m diagonal road's bbox would otherwise fence off a whole quarter. The sim's own `spotFree` already blocks by the outline / the stroke's width, not the bbox.
- A field's `perDay` does not yet scale with its drawn area (a 300 m² field feeds like an 8×6 one).
- Areas and lines are placed exactly wherever they are drawn, even off the plot or across the lake; the visual layer clips them to the land.
- The visual layer must call `game.deliverLetter(id)` for carried neighbour letters once it listens to `envoy:send` (else they land by the 25 s safety net); `agent:task kind:'journey'` needs a visual (hide, or walk toward `dir`).
- Roads do not speed anyone up; bridges and docks sit on dry ground next to water; hauling is straight-line; nations never shrink or attack; the moon has no economy (by design).
